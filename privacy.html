Privacy Policy

Workspace MCP · last updated 1 September 2026

Who this covers

Workspace MCP is a personal, single-user application. It is used only by its owner and the owner's immediate household, on the owner's own personal computer. It is not distributed to the public and has no other users.

What data the application accesses

When the owner grants consent, the application accesses data in the owner's own Google account through Google's official APIs. Depending on which features are used, this may include Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Calendar events, Google Tasks, and Google Contacts belonging to the owner. The application accesses this data only in response to a direct instruction from the owner, on the owner's own machine.

What the application does with that data

Data is read into memory on the owner's local computer, used to carry out the requested task, and then discarded. Some results are written back into the owner's own Google account — for example, updating a cell in the owner's own spreadsheet.

The application does not:

  • transmit the owner's data to any server operated by the developer;
  • sell, rent, trade, or share the owner's data with any third party;
  • use the owner's data for advertising or profiling;
  • retain copies of the owner's data beyond what the owner explicitly saves to their own computer.

Credentials and storage

OAuth tokens are stored in files on the owner's local computer, with filesystem permissions restricting access to the owner's own operating-system account. No credentials are transmitted anywhere except to Google's own authentication endpoints.

Google user data and AI models

Data obtained through Google APIs is not used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or otherwise develop any generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning model.

Limited Use

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Because data is not collected or stored by the developer, there is nothing for the developer to delete. The owner may revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently invalidates its credentials. Deleting the local token files has the same effect.

Children

This application is not directed at children and is not used by children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.
